With MausBot Cloud you have two places in one app:

- **This computer**: the OpenMausBot that runs on your Mac or PC.
- **My Cloud**: the one that keeps running when your computer is off.

You switch between them with the switcher at the top of the sidebar. Everything about the two lives in one place: **Settings → MausBot Cloud**.

## What lives where

Each place keeps its own bots, chats and settings. **Send everything to My Cloud** in **Settings → MausBot Cloud** sends what this computer has.

| | Goes to My Cloud | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Bots** (instructions, picture, settings, skills) | Yes | With Send everything |
| **A new bot's memory notes** | Yes, once | With the bot, the first time it goes |
| **Logins and 1Password** | Yes | Over a private connection, never through a chat |
| **Your name, email, About me and language** | Yes | With Send everything |
| **Chats**, in rooms too | Yes | The conversations and messages My Cloud doesn't have yet |
| **Routines and automations** | No | Both places would run them, so nothing would run once. Set them up on My Cloud. |
| **Sign-ins to Claude, ChatGPT and other engines, API keys** | No | They belong to each machine. Sign in on My Cloud itself. |
| **Connected apps** (Gmail, Calendar, Slack…) | No | Connect them on My Cloud: each side has its own connections. |
| **MCP tools** | No | Add them on My Cloud. |
| **Files** | Folders you choose | The **Files** page |

Nothing ever goes the other way by itself. What you change on My Cloud stays on My Cloud.

## How your things get to My Cloud

**Send everything to My Cloud** sends bots, skills, logins, 1Password, your settings and your chats. Sending only adds and updates: nothing is ever deleted on either side, and a long chat history goes once, then only what's new.

**Settings → MausBot Cloud** on this computer shows whether My Cloud is online and one line on where things stand: *Not on My Cloud yet: 2 bots, 1 login and 12 chats*, *Bringing chats to My Cloud… 3 of 12*, *Everything from this computer is on My Cloud*, or, if something couldn't go, what and why, with **Try again**.

A few rules keep this safe:

- **What you change on My Cloud is kept.** If you edit a bot on My Cloud, a later change on this computer never overwrites it.
- **What you remove on My Cloud stays removed,** a chat included: it isn't sent again.
- **A bot keeps its logins.** A login or a 1Password vault kept to Ember is kept to Ember on My Cloud too.
- **Engines.** A bot that uses an engine My Cloud isn't signed in to uses My Cloud's own engine instead, and Settings says so.

### On My Cloud

When this computer's things move to My Cloud, a line at the top of My Cloud in the desktop app shows them arriving, then what came, for example *12 bots, 3 skills, 2 logins, 1Password and 48 chats came over from This computer*. If something can't come over, the line says what and why, for example *1Password couldn't be sent: 1Password did not accept this computer's token on My Cloud…*, with **Try again**. Otherwise it stays out of the way.

### Your chats

Send everything brings the chats too: the conversations My Cloud doesn't have, and the newer messages of the ones it has, under the same bots and rooms. A conversation you're in the middle of goes once the bot has answered.

**Start My Cloud over from this computer**, a link at the bottom of **Settings → MausBot Cloud**, is different: it replaces everything on My Cloud with this computer's, after backing My Cloud up. Use it only to start My Cloud over.

## Sign in to Claude or ChatGPT on My Cloud

Sign-ins and API keys never leave the machine they were made on. Open My Cloud in the app, then sign in under **Settings → AI** there, or follow the setup card in its first chat.

## Let your bots use your Mac

**Let my bots use this Mac** (**this PC** on Windows) is one switch, in **Settings → MausBot Cloud** on this computer and on My Cloud's own Settings page in the desktop app, so you never have to leave My Cloud to change it. Turn it on and your bots on My Cloud open the websites that turn away data centres, such as Instagram, YouTube and Google sign-in, through this computer's internet connection, so those websites see your home connection; every other website loads straight from My Cloud, which is faster. In your own conversations and routines they can also read your Desktop, Documents and Downloads, without changing anything, while this computer is awake and the app is open. The line under the switch says what is lent, for example *Your bots can use the internet connection and Desktop, Documents and Downloads (read only) on this Mac*. While the switch is on, the app opens by itself when you log in, out of sight, and its menu-bar icon reads **In use** while your bots use the computer. When the computer is away, those websites open from My Cloud's own connection and may turn your bots away, and Reddit stays off either way.

To lend apps and screen, tick **Apps and screen** under the switch, on My Cloud's page or on this computer. The app asks you to confirm in a window of its own first. To lend other folders, let bots edit them, or stop lending the internet connection, open **Choose what's lent** under the switch on this computer. With apps and screen turned on, a bot on My Cloud can also open websites in your computer's own browser, where your usual sign-ins and passkeys work. Turn it off at any time, from either switch or from the app's icon in the menu bar or taskbar.

## Signing in to Google and other passkey sites

A passkey lives on your own phone or computer, so a browser on My Cloud can't use it. What works:

1. **Lend your computer** (above, with apps and screen) and ask the bot to use your computer's browser for that site. Approve the passkey on your computer when it asks.
2. **Try another way**: in the bot's browser, on the passkey step, choose **Try another way**, then your password and a code from your authenticator app, a text message or a backup code.
3. **For Gmail, Calendar and Drive**, connect them under **Connected apps** on My Cloud instead. The bot then doesn't need a browser sign-in at all.

Step-by-step help, and what to do when a page doesn't respond: [Signing in to Google from a cloud browser](../troubleshooting/google-sign-in-cloud-browser.mdx).

## Which version am I on?

My Cloud updates by itself, a little after each desktop release. **Settings → MausBot Cloud** shows its version next to *My Cloud is online*, and says so when it's older than this computer's. To see this computer's version, open the menu under your name at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **About**.

## Where are the logs?

- **This computer**: **Settings → General → Diagnostics → Open logs folder**. **Export Diagnostics…** saves one file with secrets removed, ready to send to support. Details: [Where are my logs?](../troubleshooting/logs.mdx).
- **My Cloud** keeps its own logs on the Cloud machine. MausBot Cloud support can read them: tell them what went wrong and roughly when.
